The brand idea

Most chatbots forget when the tab closes. Rikol is the one that doesn't. Everything about the brand should feel like a trusted notebook that can also talk.

Exact

Rikol returns what you gave it, in your own words, not a rewrite. What you saved is shown in a serif voice and marked with a highlight so you can see it's yours.

Quiet

Rikol doesn't announce that it remembers. It answers, says when the thing was saved, and stops. No performance, no pet names.

Yours

You can see, edit, and forget anything you've saved. The brand is calm because the user is in control.

ree-KOL

The name is "recall" spelled the way you would say it. Short, lowercase in the wordmark, and easy to type from memory, which suits a product that is all about remembering.

Logo

One clean chat bubble drawn in a single stroke, with its top-right corner left open. A small yellow dot sits in the gap: the thing you said, coming back.

The dot is the memory. It carries the one yellow fill in the system, the same highlighter that marks recalled text in the product. At small sizes it still reads as a chat icon with a notification-style accent.

The gap is the recall. The outline is one continuous line that stops short of the corner. It's what makes this bubble ownable, and it costs nothing at 20 px.

The wordmark

Always lowercase, set in Bricolage Grotesque Bold with tight tracking. Never capitalise the R, and never set it in the serif.

Colour

A deep violet-black base with soft lilac and one highlighter yellow. The yellow is rationed: it only ever marks something that was remembered.

Deep Well

#1F1535

Primary dark. Backgrounds, text on light, the app icon. Black with some depth in it.

Page

#F2F0F9

Primary light. A cool off-white that sits well beside lilac and never looks yellowed.

Haze

#B9A8FF

Secondary. The user's own messages, focus rings, the round avatar. It's the colour of the user's side of the conversation.

Quiet

#5A5472

Secondary text and captions on light backgrounds. About 6:1 contrast on Page.

Pin

#FFD84A

The highlighter. Recalled text, the front bubble, the primary action. Always paired with Deep Well text, never white.

Aim for roughly these proportions. If Pin starts to fill more than a few percent of a screen, it stops meaning "this was remembered" and starts being decoration.

Motion

One signature move. When Rikol hands something back, the highlight sweeps across it from left to right, like a marker going over a line.

Sweep, 700 ms

Ease-out, left to right, once per recalled snippet. It plays when the answer appears and not again.

Everything else stays still
